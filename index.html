The Rules

How to get hired

1

Can we hear you?

This is a panel interview. In real life, many people interview you at once.

Today, the class is your panel. If they can't hear you, you can't be hired.

2

10 seconds minimum

An elevator pitch is 30 seconds. You're lucky: you only need 10.

Under 10 seconds = you can't be hired.

3

Something new

If I already heard it, it doesn't count.

Your group's idea was taken? Your own idea is better? Try it anyway.

One round

  1. 1 minute: think and write.
  2. 1 minute: interview your partner. Ask "Why should I pick you?" Then switch.
  3. Each group sends one applicant.
  4. Walk-in applicants: anyone with something new can stand up and try.
  5. Each group hires someone (not from your own group) and says why. Your reason must be new too.
  6. The hired person's group becomes the panel next round. No prep. You ask the questions.
Example

Job: Dog Walker

Not hired4 seconds
"You should pick me because I like dogs. I am kind."
Hiredabout 15 seconds
"You should pick me because I'm not scared of big dogs. For example, last summer my neighbor's husky pulled me 50 meters down the street, and I never let go of the leash. After that, I walked him every day. Now he runs to me every time he sees me."
